---
name: NorthPeak visual direction
description: Durable visual direction for the NorthPeak Digital agency site.
---

NorthPeak Digital should stay dark by default and feel like a premium editorial SaaS agency: near-black surfaces, restrained violet gradients, high-contrast light typography, generous whitespace, rounded glass surfaces, and subtle motion.

**Why:** The attached creative brief explicitly positioned the site alongside Linear, Framer, Raycast, Vercel, and Stripe, and the refined direction was verified successfully at desktop and mobile sizes.

**How to apply:** Preserve the dark/violet system, the spacious 1280px-centered composition, recruiter-facing polish, and restrained animation when adding or revising sections.

Performance work should prefer loading and rendering optimizations over visual simplification so the premium presentation remains intact.

**Why:** The Lighthouse optimization brief explicitly required preserving the existing layout, content, animations, and design while improving loading and accessibility scores.

**How to apply:** Keep the current visual elements; favor font-request consolidation, contained below-the-fold rendering, reduced-motion safeguards, semantic labels, and removal of unused app-shell dependencies.
